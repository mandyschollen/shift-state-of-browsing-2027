Summary

Google Chrome still dominates desktop market share, but privacy anxieties and AI skepticism are creating opportunities for alternative browsers.

AllowNot now Ask first

Younger adults are struggling more with digital fatigue, but both younger and older generations prefer browsers that give them more control over automated decision-making, even though a majority have used AI search tools. The findings produce a clear message for browsers: make browsing smarter without making people feel like they have surrendered control.

Privacy Transparency Permissions

Most people appear receptive to AI when it helps them research, organize and navigate information, but transparency, privacy protections, and user choice may be just as important to adoption as the underlying technology.

About the 2027 State of Browsing Report

This survey was conducted among 1,005 U.S. internet users in September, 2026 by MX8 Labs. The sample is nationally representative across age, gender, income, and region.

Shift Browser commissioned and produced the 2027 State of Browsing Report to understand what people actually want from their browser, not what the industry assumes they want.
